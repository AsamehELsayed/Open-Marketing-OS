## Client-data limitation

The Northstar Business Profile and its derived Brief already assert “small Maadi café” / Maadi as the business location. The retained raw project note only says beans are roasted in Maadi and separately that the café hosts a weekly tasting; it does not tie the café location to Maadi. This remains an unresolved client-data issue. No Business Profile, Brief, project knowledge, campaign, or other client record was edited. The prompt and validator cannot independently correct an inaccurate fact that is explicitly present in the selected Business Profile/Brief; that stored source needs a later user-authorized data correction. The regression fixture intentionally removes the profile assertion when testing the unsupported combination.

The added location check is a deterministic wording heuristic, not semantic proof. It requires a detected establishment/location statement to match one allowed source and does not establish whether any source itself is true. Real-model behavior has not been verified.
